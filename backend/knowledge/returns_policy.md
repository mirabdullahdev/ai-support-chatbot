# Returns and Exchanges Policy

## 30-day returns
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition with all tags attached.

## How to return an item
1. Use the prepaid return label included in your package, or request a new one from our support team with your order number.
2. Pack the items in any box or the original mailer and attach the label.
3. Drop the parcel off at any carrier location. We email you as soon as the return is scanned.

## Return shipping costs
- Domestic (United States) returns are free with our prepaid label.
- International returns are the customer's responsibility, and original shipping fees are not refunded. We recommend a tracked service.
- A $5.95 return label fee is deducted from the refund if you ask us to generate a label for a domestic return that does not use the included prepaid label.

## Refund timing
Once your return arrives at our warehouse we inspect it within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days. Original shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong product.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or colour are free. Start an exchange from the return label request, and we ship the new item as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier, so you do not need to wait for the refund cycle. If the item you want is out of stock we will refund you or offer store credit.

## Store credit
Gift returns, and returns made after the 30-day window but within 60 days, are eligible for store credit instead of a refund. Store credit never expires.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked Final Sale at checkout.
- Gift cards.
- Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If something arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or a full refund including shipping, and you do not need to return the item.
