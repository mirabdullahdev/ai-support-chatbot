# Shipping Policy

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2pm Pacific Time on a business day are processed and handed to the carrier the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day. During sale events processing can take up to 2 business days.

## Domestic shipping (United States)
We ship to all 50 states, plus APO/FPO addresses.

- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.95.
- Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95.
- Overnight shipping takes 1 business day and costs $24.95 (order before 12pm Pacific Time).
- Free standard shipping on all domestic orders over $50 (after discounts).

Express and overnight shipping are not available to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.

## International shipping
We ship to more than 40 countries. Rates are flat per order and shown at checkout.

- Canada: 5 to 8 business days, $12.00.
- United Kingdom and European Union: 7 to 12 business days, $15.00.
- Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Singapore: 10 to 14 business days, $18.00.
- Free international shipping on orders over $150.

Duties and taxes: Orders to Canada, the UK and the EU are shipped duties paid (DDP), so there are no surprise fees on delivery. For other countries, customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer and are collected by the carrier on delivery.

We do not currently ship to countries under trade sanctions, or to freight forwarders outside the United States.

## Order tracking
You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created. International orders are tracked until they reach the destination country's local carrier.

## Delivery problems
- Lost packages: if your tracking has not updated in 7 business days (14 for international orders), contact us and we will open a carrier investigation and send a replacement or refund.
- Damaged packages: contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we will send a replacement at no cost.
- Wrong address: if a package is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we will re-ship it once the shipping cost is covered.
- Split shipments: items that are in stock together usually ship in one box. If an order is split you are only charged shipping once.
