# Nova Threads FAQ

## General

### What is Nova Threads?
Nova Threads is an online clothing store for modern everyday basics. We design relaxed, well-made pieces in natural fabrics like cotton, linen and merino wool, and ship them from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

### How can I contact customer support?
You can chat with this assistant any time. A human team member is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Pacific Time, and replies to emails at support@novathreads.example within one business day.

### Do you have physical stores?
No, Nova Threads is online only. That is how we keep prices fair and quality high.

## Orders and payment

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We also offer Shop Pay installments on orders over $75.

### Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, as long as it is still in "Processing" status. After it has shipped we cannot change it, but you can return it for free within 30 days.

### Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
Yes, if the order is still in "Processing" status. Contact the team with your order number and the new address. Once an order has shipped the address cannot be changed.

### How do I track my order?
As soon as your order ships we email you a tracking number and a tracking link. You can also ask this assistant, "Where is my order?" and provide your order number and email.

### Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Digital gift cards from $25 to $250 are delivered by email instantly and never expire.

### Do you offer discount codes or student discounts?
We run seasonal sales and send subscribers a 10% welcome code. We also offer a 15% student discount through our verification partner. Discount codes cannot be combined.

## Products and sizing

### How do your sizes fit?
Most Nova Threads pieces have a relaxed, true-to-size fit. If you are between sizes we recommend sizing up for tops and outerwear, and sizing down for the Slim Stretch Chinos. Each product page includes a size guide.

### What materials do you use?
We favor natural, durable fabrics: organic cotton, European linen, merino wool and recycled fleece. Each product lists its exact composition and care instructions.

### Are your clothes ethically made?
Our factories are audited annually for fair wages and safe working conditions, and our cotton is certified organic. We publish our supplier list every year on our website.

### Will out-of-stock items be restocked?
Popular items are usually restocked within 4 to 6 weeks. You can join the waitlist on any sold-out product page and we will email you the moment it is back.

## Shipping and returns

### Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to more than 40 countries including Canada, the UK, the EU, Australia and Japan. International delivery takes 5 to 14 business days depending on the destination. See the shipping policy for rates.

### What is your return policy?
You can return any unworn item with tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or an exchange. Domestic returns are free with the prepaid label we include in every box.
